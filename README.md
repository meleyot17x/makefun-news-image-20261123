THE DAY'S POST — a one-page broadsheet that sums up Monday 23 November 2026 in 41 stories across 8 regions. Twelve numbered main cards with original drawings, certainty chips and sources are led by the US Army plan to detonate unstable explosives in Iowa, with the IRGC Patriot-radar claim in Bahrain and the COP31 close in Antalya completing the lead trio. A Day in Numbers rail, a dotted world map with numbered pins, a UTC timeline and a red Date Check box (nine dated stories left out) complete the page. `compose.py` draws the page; `main.py` copies the finished `entry.png` byte for byte.
